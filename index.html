Hello, I'm Nilesh

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of California San Diego, where I focus on making machine learning systems more efficient for real-world deployment. My research sits at the intersection of inference efficiency, model compression, and hardware-algorithm co-design.

Before starting my PhD, I spent three years at Qualcomm AI Research as a Senior Machine Learning Engineer, where I worked on parameter-efficient fine-tuning and low-bit quantization for deploying generative models on edge devices. My work there led to multiple publications at top venues including NeurIPS, CVPR, and ICCV.

I received my Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UC San Diego (2021) and my Bachelor's degree in Engineering Physics from IIT Guwahati (2019).

Research Interests: Efficient LLM Training & Inference, Speculative Decoding, Post-Training Quantization, Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT), Hardware-Algorithm Co-design
